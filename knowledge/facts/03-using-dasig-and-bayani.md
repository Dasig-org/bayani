# Facts: using Dasig, privacy and Bayani

## start-a-group
Q: How do I start a group? | How long does setup take? | How do I set up teams?
A: About five minutes, on one phone, with no internet. Open Dasig and give a nickname, choose "I run a group", name it and pick its kind and positions, then start a project and pick the teams it needs, or start from last year's teams. Open a team and tap Lead to choose its lead.
Source: https://bayanihan.dasig.org/docs/start-a-group
Updated: 2026-11-14

## bring-people-in
Q: How do I add people to my group? | How do volunteers join? | Do volunteers need the app?
A: Together in one place, make one phone the group's hub: others join its hotspot and scan its QR code, with no internet. Apart, send an invite link. Without the app, add someone by nickname so you can record their work; they can claim it later. A nickname is all a volunteer needs to join.
Source: https://bayanihan.dasig.org/docs/start-a-group
Updated: 2026-11-14

## volunteering
Q: What can volunteers do? | What is "I'm in"? | What is a good first step?
A: Volunteers use the same app as coordinators. Today shows what you've taken, what someone asked you to do, and open work you can say "I'm in" to; some work is marked as a good first step for newcomers. You can suggest work, and your team's lead accepts it and credits you for the idea.
Source: https://bayanihan.dasig.org/docs/volunteering
Updated: 2026-11-14

## your-story
Q: Do I keep a record of my volunteering? | What is "Your story"? | What happens to my work if I leave?
A: Everything you finish is signed by the group that received it and kept on your phone, across every group you help. Download it any time from Me → Your data. Leaving a group never erases what you did; deleting your account removes your profile after 14 days, and groups keep the work credited to "a former volunteer".
Source: https://bayanihan.dasig.org/docs/volunteering
Updated: 2026-11-14

## offline-and-hubs
Q: Does Dasig need the internet? | Does it work without signal or data? | What is a hub? | Can we use it where there's no signal?
A: Dasig never needs the internet to do the work. Bayanihan Server runs inside the app, so one person can run a project on their own phone. A group can make one phone or tablet its hub: others join its hotspot and scan its QR code once, and their phones save to it whenever they're near. Changes made away from the hub are sent, in order, when back in range.
Source: https://bayanihan.dasig.org/docs/hubs
Updated: 2026-11-14

## hub-backup
Q: What if the hub phone is lost? | How do I back up my group's records?
A: One hub phone holds everything, so if it's lost its records go with it. Keep it safe and download your group's records now and then (group page → Download). When the Dasig Ecosystem opens, a group will be able to keep a copy on its server too.
Source: https://bayanihan.dasig.org/docs/hubs
Updated: 2026-11-14

## privacy
Q: Where is my data stored? | Who can see my group's records? | Do you sell data?
A: The Dasig app keeps your group's records on your phones or your group's hub phone; nothing is sent to Dasig unless your group joins the Dasig Ecosystem, whose server is in Singapore. Your group decides who sees what. There's no advertising and no selling or renting of data. The people a group serves are counted, never named.
Source: https://bayanihan.dasig.org/trust/privacy
Updated: 2026-11-14

## ai-training-data
Q: Is my group's data used to train AI? | Does Bayani learn from our records?
A: No, not unless a group opts in. Bayani reads your group's records on the phone to make suggestions, but those records aren't used to train any model without the group's agreement.
Source: https://bayanihan.dasig.org/trust/privacy
Updated: 2026-11-14

## safety-reports
Q: How do I report a problem? | What if a group is fake? | Something looks wrong on the Ecosystem
A: Report it, even without an account: fraudulent or impersonating groups, hateful or sexual content, anything that puts someone in danger, especially a child, or your own information shown where you didn't agree. During the pilot every report is read within 2 working days. If someone is in immediate danger, call 911 or your local barangay first.
Source: https://bayanihan.dasig.org/trust/safety
Updated: 2026-11-14

## what-is-bayani
Q: Who are you? | What is Bayani? | Are you an AI?
A: I'm Bayani, the small AI model in the Dasig app, in development. I help your group remember what its projects usually need, and suggest smaller steps, missing steps and what usually comes first. I run on the phone, I only suggest, and a person decides every change.
Source: https://bayanihan.dasig.org/bayani
Updated: 2026-11-14

## bayani-how-it-works
Q: How does Bayani know what my project needs? | Where does Bayani get its suggestions?
A: From your group's own finished projects. Each kind of step your group does is remembered as a Practice, with how often it was needed, when it was usually done, what came before it and how often it was forgotten until the last week. Dasig's rules count those; Bayani judges whether a step is the same as one you know and whether it applies to this project.
Source: https://bayanihan.dasig.org/docs/bix
Updated: 2026-11-14

## bayani-limits
Q: Can Bayani do anything else? | Can you write a letter for us? | Can I chat with Bayani?
A: Bayani isn't a chatbot. It answers questions about your group's projects and steps, from your group's own records, and says when something is outside what it can help with. Every change it suggests is a draft that a person accepts or declines.
Source: https://bayanihan.dasig.org/bayani
Updated: 2026-11-14

## bayani-choose
Q: Can we turn Bayani off? | Who decides what Bayani helps with?
A: Each group chooses. An officer turns on only the areas the group wants, such as remembering how projects go or breaking down steps, and everything is off until then. The app works the same without Bayani.
Source: https://bayanihan.dasig.org/bayani
Updated: 2026-11-14

## bayani-privacy
Q: Does Bayani send my data anywhere? | Does Bayani need the internet? | Can Bayani see everything?
A: Bayani runs on the phone and needs no internet; nothing is sent anywhere to get an answer. It only reads what the person using it may see, so its suggestions never show more than they could open themselves.
Source: https://bayanihan.dasig.org/bayani
Updated: 2026-11-14
