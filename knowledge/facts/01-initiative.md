# Facts: the initiative and its parts

## what-is-bayanihan
Q: What is Bayanihan? | What does Bayanihan mean here? | What are the Bayanihan foundations?
A: Bayanihan is the Filipino tradition of a community lifting a neighbour's house together. Here it also names the open foundations anyone can use and build on: BIX (the open format), Bayanihan Core (the rules engine), Bayanihan Server (the reference server) and Bayanihan Intelligence (the AI programme, whose model is Bayani).
Source: https://bayanihan.dasig.org/about
Updated: 2026-11-14

## what-is-dasig
Q: What is Dasig? | What is the Dasig app? | What does this app do?
A: Dasig is the app for volunteers and organizations. It turns a group's work into teams and small steps, so everyone can carry a part: someone starts a project, leads break it down, and volunteers tap "I'm in". It's free, open source, mobile first, and works without the internet.
Source: https://bayanihan.dasig.org/
Updated: 2026-11-14

## who-makes-dasig
Q: Who makes Dasig? | Who made this app? | Who is behind this? | Who is Dasig Inc.?
A: Dasig is made by Dasig Inc., which also develops BIX and runs Dasig Fundraising. "Dasig" and the hands emblem are trademarks of Dasig Inc., the way Firefox is Mozilla's: anyone can use the open code, but only Dasig Inc. ships it as Dasig.
Source: https://bayanihan.dasig.org/about
Updated: 2026-11-14

## dasig-vs-dasig-fundraising
Q: Is this the same as dasig.org? | How is Dasig different from Dasig Fundraising? | Can I raise money here?
A: They are separate. Dasig Fundraising, at dasig.org, is where people raise money. The Dasig app is where groups coordinate the work. Raising money stays on Dasig Fundraising; coordinating the work lives in the Dasig app. Their data is kept separately.
Source: https://bayanihan.dasig.org/about
Updated: 2026-11-14

## who-it-is-for
Q: Who is Dasig for? | What kind of groups use it? | Is it only for nonprofits?
A: It's for the groups that do community work, registered or not: clubs, parishes and faith communities, school organizations, barangay youth, event teams, and the volunteers around them. It's built for the ordinary week, like a feeding program, tutoring or a cleanup. Disaster response is one kind of work it can hold, not its premise.
Source: https://bayanihan.dasig.org/
Updated: 2026-11-14

## why-it-exists
Q: Why was this built? | What problem does it solve? | Why do groups need this?
A: Groups already do the work. What's hard is everything around it: chasing replies in chats, not knowing who's bringing what, receipts and photos lost in phones, the way things were done leaving when officers change, and writing the same report in four formats. Bayanihan works because someone does the extra work; Dasig is for sharing it.
Source: https://bayanihan.dasig.org/
Updated: 2026-11-14

## status-pilot
Q: Is Dasig available? | Can I download the app? | Is it on Google Play or the App Store?
A: Dasig is in pilot with a small number of groups, who get the app directly with help setting up. The Android app is in pilot now and goes to Google Play after the pilot; the iPhone app is being prepared for the App Store. Groups can ask to join the pilot at hello@dasig.org.
Source: https://bayanihan.dasig.org/get
Updated: 2026-11-14
